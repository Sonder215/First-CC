2025级本科生 · Undergraduate, Class of 2025

I believe that language is not merely a tool for communication — it is the medium through which we construct our understanding of the world, of others, and of ourselves. As a student of English at Tianjin University of Science and Technology, I am on a journey to explore not just the mechanics of a foreign tongue, but the literature, culture, and ways of thinking that it carries within it. Each word I learn is a window; each book I read, a door.

Academic Journey

2025

Began undergraduate studies in the English Department, School of Foreign Languages. Embarked on a four-year journey into the depths of English language, literature, and culture.

2025–2026

Foundation Year · 基础年

Building a solid foundation in English phonetics, intensive reading, extensive reading, and basic writing. Discovering the joy of reading English literature in the original.

Diving deeper into linguistics, British and American literature, translation studies, and academic writing. Beginning to develop my own voice in English.

Focusing on advanced topics in literary criticism, comparative literature, and professional translation. Preparing for the future, whatever it may hold.

Looking forward to completing my B.A. in English, equipped with linguistic proficiency, cultural awareness, and a lifelong love of learning.
